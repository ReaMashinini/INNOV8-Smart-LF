The Process

How INNOV8 works

Simple, fast, and safe. No app required for the finder. No complex setup for you.

1

Register your account

Sign up and complete your profile with your name, contact details, and emergency contacts. Takes 2 minutes.

2

Order your tags

Choose from NFC stickers, QR cards, keychains, or bulk packs. We ship to your door within 3–5 business days.

3

Attach & activate

Each tag has a unique code. Log in, activate it, and link it to any item — bag, keys, laptop, water bottle.

4

Finder scans or taps

Someone finds your item. They tap their phone on the NFC or scan the QR. Your profile page opens instantly.

5

You're reunited! 🎉

The finder contacts you directly or sends an anonymous message. You get notified and recover your item.

NFC vs QR — We use both

Every SmartLF tag includes both technologies for maximum compatibility.

📡

NFC Technology

Near Field Communication — the same tech in your bank card and Apple Pay.

  • ✅ No camera needed
  • ✅ Works in 0.5 seconds
  • ✅ Very intuitive ("tap and go")
  • ⚠️ Older phones may not support
🔲

QR Technology

Quick Response codes — scannable with any smartphone camera. Universal.

  • ✅ Works on ALL smartphones
  • ✅ No special hardware
  • ✅ Universal fallback
  • ⚠️ Needs camera + lighting

Our Story

Built by students, for students

SmartLF was born at a university campus in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal after one too many items went missing in residences, libraries, and taxi ranks. We asked: why is there no simple tech solution for this?

Traditional lost-and-found systems are slow, location-limited, and often never reunite owners with their belongings. AirTags and GPS trackers are expensive and battery-dependent. We wanted something cheap, durable, and instantly effective.

SmartLF uses NFC + QR technology — the same tech in your bank card — to create a digital identity for physical objects. Affordable. Battery-free. Works anywhere there's a phone.

🎯

Our Mission

To make losing things temporary — and returning them simple, safe, and instant.

🌱

Sustainability

Eco-friendly packaging. Recycled sticker materials. Reducing unnecessary replacements of lost items reduces waste.

🔐

Privacy & Safety
